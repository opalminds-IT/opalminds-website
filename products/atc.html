The Challenge

Volume grows. Staff
can't keep up.

Freight forwarders face a structural problem: customs declaration is document-intensive, error-prone, and time-critical. Legacy systems add AI features on top of manual workflows — but the bottlenecks remain.

01

Document Chase

Declarations held up waiting for documents from shippers. Manual follow-up delays every clearance cycle and compounds at volume — every pending document is a stalled shipment.

02

Manual Reconciliation

Coordinators manually reconciling invoices with packing lists. Error-prone, slow, and impossible to scale without adding headcount. Cross-document inconsistencies surface too late.

03

Post-Submission Errors

HS code errors discovered after submission — requiring costly amendments, triggering audits, and creating border delays that ripple through the entire supply chain. The most expensive place to find mistakes.

04

Peak Overwhelm

Peak periods overwhelm teams. Clearance delays cascade forward. Compliance risk scales linearly with volume — without execution automation, growth means proportional headcount growth.

The Solution

Purpose-built execution architecture — not workflow software with AI bolted on.

Our platform coordinates every step of the declaration lifecycle autonomously. Ingest documents from all channels, validate before declaration is formed, reconcile cross-document relationships, and execute with configurable human governance. AI handles the routine. Humans govern the exceptions.

Architecture

The Execution Stack

Four pillars that make autonomous execution possible — and safe.

Execution

Built for Continuous Customs Operations

Unlike systems with AI add-ons, our platform was designed for execution: coordinated workflows, continuous operations, and shipment-level intelligence. Real-time state management across all declaration stages. Automatic retry and escalation on failure.

Ingest › Validate › Reconcile › Declare
Context

Contextual Awareness Across All Documents

Maintains relationships between invoices, packing lists, and waybills — line items, shipment details, HS codes, and tariff rules — across the entire declaration lifecycle. Persistent shipment identity. Downstream impact analysis before any change.

Validation

Proactive Error Detection

Flags value mismatches, missing fields, and HS code conflicts before submission — not after. Value and quantity mismatch detection, missing required field identification, HS classification validation, and trade agreement compliance checks. Zero border-discovered errors.

Governance

Humans Govern Exceptions

Configurable approval checkpoints, confidence thresholds, and role-based workflows. High-confidence declarations execute autonomously. Edge cases escalate immediately with full context — risk score, matched rule, and recommended action. Complete audit trail.
